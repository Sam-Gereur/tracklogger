Key Principles
📐
Hole numbering — Hole 1 is stiffest (bar attaches closest to pivot). Higher numbers are softer. Your setup records the 2 attachment points: inner (chassis) and outer (arm).
⚖️
Front vs Rear — The bar with more stiffness relative to the other dominates balance. A stiffer front bar pushes toward understeer. A stiffer rear bar pushes toward oversteer.
🔁
Change one at a time — Always change front OR rear, never both in the same session. Establish a baseline, then tune from there.
🌡️
Tyre temps tell the story — After a session, compare inner vs outer tyre temps. Too much outer heat = excessive roll = consider stiffer bar. Uniform temps = good platform.
🛞
Sway bars vs coilovers — Coilovers affect both cornering and straight-line compliance. Sway bars only affect roll stiffness (not single-wheel bumps). Tune coilovers for ride quality, sway bars for balance.
